Three days before Christmas, we light the last candle of Advent: love. And in a way, it's the candle the others were always pointing to. We have hope because God loves us. We have peace because God loves us. We have joy because God loves us. Love is the reason for all of it.

But "God loves you" is a sentence we've heard so often it can go flat. Christmas is God's way of making sure it never stays abstract.

## Love with skin on

We often shrink love down to a feeling. God turned it into an event. Scripture gives us the whole gospel in a single, famous line: "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."

Look at the verbs. God so loved that he gave. Love, in the Bible, is something you do, at cost, for someone else's good. And what God gave wasn't a card or a sentiment; he gave his Son. The manger is love with skin on, love that left heaven, love that came close.

## God with us

One of the names given to Jesus at Christmas is Immanuel, which means "God with us." Sit with that for a second. Not God watching from a distance. Not God disappointed and keeping his distance. God with us, in the mess, in the flesh, in a barn in a backwater town, because he wanted to be near the people he made.

And here's the part that undoes me every year: he came before we cleaned ourselves up. He didn't wait for us to get it together first. Love came down while we were still a mess, which means you never have to become presentable before you come to him. You come as you are; that was the whole point.

## The love we then carry

A church that has been loved like this can't help but love. It's why we say "there's room for you," and mean it. It's why we serve our city and welcome the stranger. We're not the source of the love; we're just people passing along what we've received. The same Spirit who fills us fills us with this, the love of God poured into ordinary hearts.

## Come and see

If you've always felt like you were on the outside of God's love, looking in, Christmas is the announcement that the door is open, and always was. Love came down for you, personally, by name.

This Sunday, the last before Christmas, we light the candle of love. Come and be loved, no performance required. Come as you are, text Pastor Brian, and let's celebrate the God who came near.
