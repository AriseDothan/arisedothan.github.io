January 4–24, 2027

A new year, on our knees

For three weeks each January, Foursquare churches across the world set aside the first days of the year to pray and fast together. This year we're joining them, and we'd love for you to join us.

The theme is “Learning to pray like Jesus.” We'll look at how Jesus prayed, the prayer he taught his friends, and the way he still prays for us, and we'll ask him to teach us to pray like that too.

How it works

Simple, and for everyone

No experience needed. Whether prayer comes easily to you or feels brand new, there's room for you in these 21 days.

A little each day

Each day has a short Scripture, a devotional, and a few prayer points, enough to steady your day, not fill it. Foursquare provides a free guide, including a Family Moments guide for praying with kids.

Prayer & fasting

Fasting simply means setting something aside, a meal, a habit, a scroll, to make more room for God. It's an invitation, never a requirement. Start small, and there's a free fasting guide if you'd like help.

Together, from wherever you are

Pray along from home, on your own or with your family, knowing the whole Arise family and churches worldwide are praying too. Text Pastor Brian any time to share a request or ask a question, we're in this together.

How to take part

Four simple steps

You don't need to sign up for anything complicated or be anywhere at a certain time. Here's all it takes.

  1. 1

    Sign up here

    Add your name below. We'll send you a reminder before we begin on January 4 so the day doesn't slip past, and so we can pray with you through the three weeks.

  2. 2

    Get your guide

    Download the free devotional or pick up the book, there's one for adults and a Family Moments version for praying with kids. Everything you need is in the next section.

  3. 3

    Pray each day, January 4–24

    Each day, read the short Scripture and devotional and pray through the prayer points in your own words. Ten unhurried minutes is plenty, wherever you are.

  4. 4

    Add a fast, if you'd like

    Completely optional. Setting aside a meal, a habit, or the phone for a while makes more room for God. There's a simple fasting guide below if you want help getting started.

Questions along the way? Text Pastor Brian any time, a real person reads every message.
