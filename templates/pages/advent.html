The study

The Marvel and Miracle of Advent

Advent is the church's ancient, counter-cultural way of slowing down to remember what Christmas is really about: a coming. God, coming to us.

Together we'll walk through The Marvel and Miracle of Advent by Christine Caine and Lisa Harper, following the four great words of the season, hope, peace, joy, and love, and letting them do their slow work in us as we wait for the one who always comes.

What it's like

An honest picture

No stage, no performance, no pressure to say a word. Just a warm room and good company as we open the season together.

Watch & talk it over

Each week we watch a short teaching video together, then talk it over, the video, the Scriptures, and whatever questions it stirs up. It's a conversation, not a lecture.

A free participant guide

We provide a participant guide as a gift, there's nothing to buy. It doubles as a devotional, with something short to work through on your own between gatherings if you'd like to go deeper. It's a gift, not homework.

Come as you are

No church background or Bible knowledge needed, and nothing to prepare, just bring yourself and your questions. Children are welcome, bring the whole family.

Four Tuesdays

Four weeks, four candles

We light one candle each week and let it lead us toward Christmas.

Week 1 · Dec 1

Hope

The sure hope that arrived in a manger, and is coming again.

Week 2 · Dec 8

Peace

The peace of God that steadies us in a noisy, hurried season.

Week 3 · Dec 15

Joy

A deep gladness that doesn't depend on everything going right.

Week 4 · Dec 22

Love

The love that came down at Christmas, and never leaves.
